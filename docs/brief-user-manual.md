## Part 1: Public website

### Getting around

- **Menu:** at the top of every page. Links to Home, About Us, Services, Gallery & Events, Partners and Contact Us.
- **On a phone:** tap the **☰** button to open the menu.
- **Footer:** at the bottom of every page. Has the contact details and the Privacy Policy link.
- **Cookie notice:** shows at the bottom on the first visit. The site only uses essential cookies, so clicking it away is all that is needed.

### The pages

#### Home
- Hero banner
- A grid of the services offered
- A short About Us section
- The partner logos

#### About Us
- Our Story, Mission and Vision. Click a button to switch between them.
- The Meet Our Team section

#### Services
- A rotating services wheel
- Service cards that slide up as you scroll
- The Consulting Package. Its button opens the contact form with the request already filled in.

#### Gallery & Events
- A photo carousel, Past Events and Upcoming Events
- Click an event to see its details in a popup.
- **View all** opens the full list of upcoming or past events.
- Links to the Cyber Young Minds page

#### Cyber Young Minds (CYM)
- Programme intro, photos and stats
- A **Register** button. The registration form asks for guardian details when the participant is under 18.

#### Campaigns
- Campaign cards showing the amount raised against the goal
- Click a card for details, then **Donate**.
- **View all** adds search, filter and sort.

#### Partners
- The partner logos and a card for each partner
- Clicking a card opens the partner's website.

#### Contact Us
- Phone, email and address
- An office map. It loads when the visitor asks for it.
- The enquiry form

#### Privacy Policy
- The privacy notice and consent wording

### How donations work

1. The visitor picks a campaign, enters an amount and their details, and is sent to **PayFast** to pay.
2. The donation only counts towards the campaign total **once PayFast confirms the payment**. Cancelled or unpaid donations are never added.
3. When the visitor returns to the site, they see whether the payment went through, is still being confirmed or was cancelled.

### What happens when visitors fill in a form

| Form              | What it creates on the staff side         |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Contact form      | An **enquiry** for Customer Service       |
| CYM registration  | A **registration** for Customer Service   |

Both forms:
- check phone numbers (South African, 10 digits)
- stop spam
- make visitors wait a short while before sending another form

> **Note:** after a staff member makes a change, the public pages update straight away. Otherwise some lists (campaigns, partners, programmes) refresh every few minutes.

## Part 2: Staff portal

### Logging in

1. Go to the staff link and log in with your username or email and password.
2. **On first login** (or after an admin resets your password) you will only see a "set up your account" form. Enter your name and choose a new password to continue.
3. Passwords must be changed **every 6 months**. A countdown shows in your profile.

### The screen layout

After logging in, you are taken to the dashboard for your role.

- **Sidebar (left):** only shows the pages your role can use.
- **Bell (top):** shows new notifications.
- **Profile picture (top right):** opens your profile and the log out button.

### The dashboards

| Role                 | What the dashboard shows |
| -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Admin**            | Active enquiries, active campaigns, total funds raised, open enquiries, registrations by interest and campaign progress |
| **Marketing**        | Active campaigns, donation totals, upcoming events, the campaigns closest to their goal and the next five events |
| **Customer Service** | New and open enquiries, registrations from the last 7 days, open enquiries and the latest registrations |

### Staff pages

#### Enquiries
Messages from the Contact Us form.

1. New enquiries show as **unclaimed**.
2. A staff member claims one and contacts the customer.
3. They then close it.

Closed enquiries have their own page and can be reopened.

#### Registrations
CYM sign ups.

- Search, filter and sort the list.
- Click one to view all details.
- Customer Service can archive. Only Admin can restore.

#### Campaigns
- Add, edit, close or archive campaigns and upload photos.
- **Only Active campaigns appear on the website and take donations.** Drafts stay hidden.
- The first photo is the campaign's main image.
- Open a campaign to see its progress and donations.

#### Gallery & Events
- Add or edit events with their date, details and photos.
- Events show as upcoming or past on the website automatically, based on their date.
- An event with no date shows as "to be confirmed".

#### Pages & Site info
Five tabs:
1. CYM programmes
2. CYM photos
3. Partners
4. Services
5. Site details (phone, email, address)

Items can be added, edited, hidden or reordered.

#### Accounts
- Create staff accounts.
- Change roles, emails, status and passwords.
- Archive people who have left.

> **Important:** a new account gets a temporary password that is shown **once**. Copy it before leaving the page.

#### Archive
Everything that has been archived (enquiries, registrations, partners, campaigns, events) in one place.

- **Restore** brings an item back.
- **Delete** removes it permanently and cannot be undone.

#### Activity Log
A read only record of everything staff have done.

- Filter by person, category, date or text.
- Download it as a CSV (Excel) file.

#### Profile
- Your own name, photo and password
- Your last 10 actions
- Only an admin can change a username, email, role or status.

## Quick tips

- **Archive, don't delete.** Archived items can always be restored. Deleting from the Archive page is permanent.
- If something you changed does not show on the website, refresh the page.
- Any problems with logging in? An admin can reset the password from the **Accounts** page.
